The gut–vagus–brain loop

Gut bacteria don't ship neurotransmitters to the brain. They control precursor supply, barrier integrity, and — through the vagus nerve — the signaling tone that decides whether the brain can make and regulate its own.

Schematic, not to scale. Cleft ≈ 20 nm; a vesicle holds ~10³–10⁴ dopamine molecules. DAT reuptake dominates striatal clearance; MAO-B/COMT dominate in prefrontal cortex. The vagus is ~80% afferent (gut→brain); gut serotonin does not cross the blood-brain barrier — its influence is via precursors, inflammation, and vagal signaling. Scenario overlays are qualitative.
